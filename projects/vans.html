Vans

Property of the shop.

A spec campaign that gives every independent skate shop its own checkerboard, and hands the brand back to the people who built it. Presented the way an agency would: brief, insight, idea, system, executions, the moment, earned story, rollout and measurement.

Creative direction · Campaign idea · Design system · Deck design · Art direction of generated photography
01 / The briefInsight, idea, direction.

The core shop
is closing.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The brand

Vans earned its credibility in independent skate shops. Most of its volume now moves through big retail and online, and the shops that made it matter are disappearing one lease at a time.

The insight

Nobody says “I skate Vans.” They say the shop. The shop is the identity; the shoe is the uniform. A campaign about the shoe competes with every sneaker ad. A campaign about the shop has no competition.

The idea

Property of the shop. Every independent shop that stocks Vans gets a checkerboard of its own: classic black and white at the toe, the shop’s colour from the heel back, the shop’s name in the brand’s type. All of them drop on Go Skateboarding Day, with no announcement from Vans. The shops post first; Vans only reposts.

01

One rule, infinite shops.

Toe stays classic, heel goes local, name in the same type. A generator turns a shop name and a colour into a full kit in a day.

02

No media buy.

One hand-painted wall per city, a film cut from shop footage, and social frames the shops post themselves. Paid for by redirected retail co-op.

03

The owner is the spokesperson.

The earned story is “our shop got its own Vans,” pitched city by city. Vans is the footnote on purpose.

Self-initiated spec concept, built as a case-study deck for agency review.

What it is not

Not commissioned by Vans. No Vans trademarks are reproduced; the wordmark is set in a generic condensed grotesk and shop names are fictional. Type, system and layouts are designed; the eight photographs are generated under art direction and show no real products or premises. No client approval or measured results exist.

Concept, campaign line, design system, type, layouts and deck design: Isaac Hernandez. The eight photographs generated with AI models under his direction.
