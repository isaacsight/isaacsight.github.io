Barred From LA

Every bar, its own character.

A wanted-poster campaign for small-batch soap — one bar cut in half to show its marbled character, on sun-faded yellow with evidence tags.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Every bar, its own character.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Barred From LA makes small-batch, handmade soaps in Los Angeles, each bar poured and cut by hand so no two batches look quite the same.

The goal

Turn that variation from a footnote into the campaign: make ‘every bar is different’ the reason to buy rather than something to apologise for.

The central idea

A wanted poster. One bar, cut open, treated like evidence: halftone ink, a hard shadow, numbered tags around it. The line names the idea and the type does the shouting. Every batch is a different suspect.

01

Cut it open.

The character of a handmade bar is inside, so the hero is a cross-section. No real product is depicted; the marbling is an abstract study.

02

Newspaper ink.

Halftone dots, letterpress-pressed black type and a faded yellow stock make it feel printed, not rendered.

03

Evidence, not decoration.

Numbered tags and scrawls frame the bar like a case file. The story version stacks the type and crops in on the cut.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.

Printed, loud, witty.

A poster system: one yellow stock, one black condensed face, one hero object printed in halftone with a hard shadow. Each new batch is a new cross-section and a new tag number; everything else stays fixed.

Fixed

Brand name full-width at the top, headline left-aligned in black, CTA in a black box. Yellow stock, halftone hero.

Variable

The bar. Each batch gets its own cut and marbling, and its own evidence number.

Format rule

Landscape sets the headline beside the bar. Vertical stacks headline above and CTA below.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
