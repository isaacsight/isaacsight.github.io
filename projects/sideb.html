SIDE B

A monthly listening-night identity — a flipped-record B, cobalt and hot pink, and a poster series that gets wheat-pasted all over the neighborhood.

Brand identity · Poster series · Merch

plays first.

The brief

Create an identity for a monthly night where a whole record is played start to finish — an event that needs a poster every month, a ticket, and something people will carry home.

The response

Side B is the deep cut, the one you have to flip over for. The mark is a B drawn as a record turned over; the two inks are the two inks a cheap screen-print shop can do on one night. Every month the poster changes only its date and its record.

01

A letter that is a record.

The flipped-vinyl B is the whole identity — it works as a stamp, a sticker, a tote, and a four-foot poster.

02

Two inks, slightly off.

Cobalt and hot pink with deliberate misregistration. It looks hand-pulled because it is designed to be.

03

Poster first, everything else second.

The night lives on walls. Tickets and merch are the poster cut down, never a separate design.

One mark.
Twelve posters a year.

The B mark and the wordmark are fixed; the date, the record and the overprint density change monthly. Because it is two inks on off-white, a year of posters reads as a single series on the wall.

Fixed

Flipped-record B mark, condensed wordmark, cobalt + pink, off-white stock, visible misregistration.

Variable

Month, date, the record being played, and how much the two inks overlap.

Format rule

Poster: mark large, date small. Ticket: mark only, perforated. Tote: mark, one color.
