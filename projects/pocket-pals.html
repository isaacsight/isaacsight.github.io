Palmbot

A glossy collectible robot head whose panels pop open to show a tiny LA sunset inside.

Palmbot No. 01 is an original designer-toy character: a chunky vinyl robot head with a palm-tree antenna and a taco-truck grille for a mouth. Its hinged head panels open to show a miniature beach at sunset. It was made as a short toy-reveal loop with three product stills.

['Concept', 'Character design', 'Art direction', 'AI image and video direction', 'Edit']
01 / The briefConcept and decisions.

The idea.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The genre

Inspired by glossy toy-like 3D collectible character reveals on halftone-pop backgrounds (the genre of studio_mostro); original character and design.

The insight

Collectible toys work because of the moment of surprise when you open one. I wanted an LA character whose whole personality is that reveal: from the outside it looks like a street-food truck with a palm tree on top, and inside it keeps the city's best ten minutes, sunset at the beach.

The idea

A glossy collectible robot head whose panels pop open to show a tiny LA sunset inside.

01

Three-colour plastic

Every panel is saturated red, cobalt blue or yellow, so it reads as one moulded toy and each part that opens is easy to spot.

02

The truck as a face

The grille and headlights make the mouth and eyes, so the character reads as a robot head and as a taco truck at once, without needing any logos.

03

Hot-pink halftone stage

A flat pink field of halftone dots and tiny palm and sun icons keeps the background graphic and lets the glossy 3D toy stand out.

02 / The workFrames and cuts.
03 / The processHow it was made.
STEP 01

Closed hero

Generated the closed toy with Nano Banana 2.1 (text-to-image, 9:16), describing the panels, hinges, materials and the halftone background in detail.

STEP 02

Consistent open state

Used Nano Banana 2.1 Edit on the hero to swing the lid and side door open and place the sunset diorama inside, keeping the camera, colours and base identical. A second edit made the macro detail of the grille and hinge.

STEP 03

Start-to-end-frame animation

Animated between the closed and open stills with Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video (8 s, 9:16), with a prompt that keeps every part rigid. The first take was used. Frame checks show the blue side panel softens for about a third of a second as it swings, instead of hinging cleanly. At full speed it reads as a pop.

STEP 04

Finish

Held the open frame for a beat at the end and added the title and AI-generated mark with HTML rendered in headless Chrome, then composited in ffmpeg.

STEP 05

Second pass: closer camera

The first cut framed the toy small and locked off, which looked flat next to the toy reels it was inspired by. I re-shot both states as extreme low-angle close-ups (Nano Banana 2.1 edit) and animated the opening between them with Wan 3.0 Prime start and end frames, so the camera pushes in as the lid and door open.
