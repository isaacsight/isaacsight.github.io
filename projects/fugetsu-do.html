Fugetsu-Do

Soft since 1903.

A seasonal campaign for a 123-year-old Little Tokyo mochi shop — layered washi circles instead of product shots, and a line that carries the whole history.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Soft since 1903.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Fugetsu-Do has made mochi and manju in Little Tokyo since 1903, now under third-generation owner Brian Kito, with seasonal varieties for festivals and holidays.

The goal

Give a shop that never needed advertising a quiet, repeatable way to announce the season — New Year, spring, summer festival — without a single staged product photo.

The central idea

Mochi is softness you can hold. The campaign turns that into paper: layered washi circles, slightly translucent, in the colors of the sweets. The date in the headline does the work no imagery could — a hundred and twenty-three years in two words.

01

Paper stands in for mochi.

Abstract washi circles suggest the shapes and colors without faking a product. Real photography of the actual sweets can sit in the same frame later.

02

Let the year be the hero.

‘Soft since 1903.’ is the only claim. One restrained serif, wide tracking, nothing else to compete with the number.

03

A palette per season.

Pink and green now; red and white for New Year; deeper greens and golds for autumn. The layout holds; the circles recolor.

Circles, cream, one red mark.

A cream field, a cluster of paper circles and one small vermilion mark — the structure of a seasonal card. Change the circle colors and the line under the headline, and the shop has a post for every festival on the calendar.

Fixed

Cream field, wide-tracked serif headline, small tracked brand name, one vermilion mark, deckled paper edges.

Variable

Circle colors and arrangement by season, and the small line naming the occasion.

Format rule

Landscape clusters the circles to one side of the headline. Vertical lets them drift top-to-bottom behind it.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.

Project scope & production notes

What this study covers

Campaign idea

Created as a complimentary, self-initiated concept and shared with the business as a proposal.

What it is not

An independent concept for a real business, created without its involvement. The artwork is AI-assisted and illustrative; the paper shapes are abstract and do not depict Fugetsu-Do’s sweets or shop.

No commission, client approval or measured results exist for this work. Motion, print and email items in the rollout are proposals only.
