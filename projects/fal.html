fal

Split times.

One prompt, three image models, one API. Each poster shows the image and the inference time fal’s API reported for that exact image, like results at a track meet. The numbers are the copy, and every one of them is real.

Creative direction · Campaign concept · Copy · Motion · Edit
01 / The briefInsight, idea, direction.

Speed you can
see.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The launch

fal runs generative media models for developers through one API. Speed is the reason developers pick it, but speed is usually sold with adjectives.

The insight

Developers don’t trust adjectives, they trust numbers. A campaign for a fast platform should be a stopwatch, not a superlative.

The idea

Split times. Run one prompt through three models on fal and publish the result like a race: the image, the model, the time. The 9:16 film runs in real time, so each picture appears at the moment its model finished.

01

The number is the headline.

Each poster leads with a time, set huge. The image is the proof underneath it.

02

Same prompt, fair race.

One prompt, a red paper crane in a beam of light, run through FLUX.1 schnell, Z-Image Turbo and FLUX.1 dev. Different models, different looks, one API.

03

Footnote everything.

The times are inference only, as fal’s API reported them, without queue or network. Saying so plainly is what makes developers believe the rest.

02 / The postersThree models, one prompt.

0.45 s.
0.63 s. 2.46 s.

The fastest image arrives before you finish reading the model name. The slowest takes about two and a half seconds and looks it, with richer light. The series lets a developer see the trade-off between speed and quality at a glance.
