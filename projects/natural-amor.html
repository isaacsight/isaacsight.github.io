Natural Amor

A little care, every day.

An editorial campaign built on one enormous drop of milk-white water, a vertical headline and a sprig of lavender — the ritual, not the product.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

A little care, every day.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Natural Amor makes small-batch soaps with considered, minimal packaging, sold to people who treat washing as a small daily pleasure rather than a chore.

The goal

Give the brand an editorial voice: a campaign that reads like a magazine spread about everyday rituals, so the packaging and product can carry the final layer.

The central idea

Care is small and repeated, so the hero is the smallest thing: a single drop, made enormous and frozen mid-fall. The headline runs up the edge like a magazine spine, and a sprig of lavender holds the baseline. One quiet image, one quiet line.

01

One drop, made huge.

Nothing branded is depicted. The drop stands in for the ritual, so real packaging can be photographed into the same light later.

02

Type as spine.

The headline runs vertically in a fine high-contrast serif, which makes the layout unmistakably editorial and leaves the image alone.

03

Oat, lavender, ivory.

Muted and warm, with film grain. Nothing saturated, so the brand’s own colours lead when they arrive.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.

Quiet, editorial, singular.

A feature-spread system: one macro subject, a vertical serif headline on the spine, a tracked small-caps brand line and a small CTA at the base. Swap the drop for an approved product macro and the layout holds.

Fixed

Headline vertical on the left edge, brand line small-caps at the top, CTA at the base. Wide margins.

Variable

The subject. A drop, a lather, a bar edge, a hand — always one thing, always macro.

Format rule

Landscape gives the drop room on the right. Vertical narrows the spine and lets the drop fill the frame.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
