Flower Song

Make something unexpected.

A workshop campaign built like an art-school collage — cut paper, loud color, and permission to get it wrong.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Make something unexpected.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Flower Song LA is known for expressive, unconventional floral work and runs creative workshops alongside its studio practice.

The goal

Make the workshops feel like what they are — a creative session, not a craft class — and give each workshop date a poster-ready look.

The central idea

A workshop is where you make something you didn’t plan. The campaign uses torn and cut paper, mismatched botanical shapes and three colors that shouldn’t work together, so the design itself demonstrates the invitation in the headline.

01

Collage, not bouquet.

Cut-paper shapes with visible edges keep the imagery joyfully abstract and avoid passing off any arrangement as the studio’s real work.

02

Three colors, no hierarchy.

Cobalt, fuchsia and acid lime share the frame equally. The grey field and black type give the eye somewhere to rest.

03

Asymmetric by rule.

The headline sits off-center and the shapes crowd the corners. The story version drops the collage to the right edge so the type column stays clear.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.

Cut it out. Move it around.

The collage is a kit of parts: a dozen cut shapes that can be rearranged per workshop, per season or per post. The type stays bold and black; the shapes do the changing.

Fixed

Stacked wordmark top-left, bold sans headline, CTA bottom-right. Black type on the warm grey field.

Variable

Arrangement, density and color balance of the cut shapes. Workshop dates and themes slot in below the headline.

Format rule

Landscape lets the shapes crowd three corners. Vertical pushes them to one edge and stacks the headline in three lines.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
