Soft Ember

Slow the moment.

A mood-led collection campaign — a sun disappearing behind linen hills, and a line about taking your time.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Slow the moment.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Soft Ember pours small-batch candles and organizes its collections by mood rather than by fragrance family.

The goal

Give the mood-based collections a campaign language that feels like the moods themselves, so each new drop can arrive as a scene rather than a scent list.

The central idea

Slowing down is the product. The hero shows the exact second the sun goes behind the hill — the moment people light a candle — rendered in soft painterly gradients so it feels like a feeling, not a place.

01

Paint, don’t photograph.

Painterly gradients and linen texture keep the imagery clearly illustrative and let future collections change the sky without reshooting anything.

02

Let the headline breathe.

A delicate oversized serif, centered, with the sun just below it. The CTA sits small at the base so the composition stays quiet.

03

A palette per mood.

Dusty blue and dusk peach for this scene. The same hills can be re-colored for a morning, a rainy afternoon or a late-night collection.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.
Dusk blue#7B93AD
Peach#F0B094
Ivory#F5EFE4
Slate#3F4E5C

One scene. Many skies.

The scene is the system: hills, sun, headline. Each mood collection changes the gradient and the line. Over a year the drops read as a series, which is the whole point of mood-based collections.

Fixed

Tracked brand name at the top, serif headline centered in the upper third, CTA at the base. Linen texture on every surface.

Variable

Sky color, position of the sun and the headline. Each collection gets its own hour of the day.

Format rule

Landscape keeps the headline on one row above the horizon. Vertical stacks it and raises the horizon.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
