Jessica Alpert

Rooms that know you.

A new-client campaign for a Studio City interior design practice — a floor plan with one room lit from within.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Rooms that know you.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Jessica Alpert Design is an interior design studio in Studio City, founded in 2019, working from new builds through to furnishing.

The goal

Give a studio whose work is bespoke a campaign that doesn’t show other people’s houses — something for new-client inquiries and project-launch posts that feels like the studio, not a portfolio.

The central idea

The plan is where every project starts; the lit room is where it ends. A fine graphite floor plan, most of it faint, with one room glowing amber as if someone just turned on a lamp. The headline promises the part of design that isn’t visible in a photograph.

01

Plan, not portfolio.

A drawn floor plan never shows a client’s home and never fabricates an interior. It shows the studio’s way of thinking instead.

02

One lit room.

Amber light in a single room is the whole emotional move — the moment a drawing becomes somebody’s evening.

03

Brass, graphite, linen.

A hairline brass rule, pencil greys and a cream linen texture. Materials a designer would actually specify.

Draw the plan. Light one room.

Every project post can use the same device: that project’s plan, the room that mattered most lit up, a line about it. For inquiries, a generic plan and the headline. The studio gets a visual signature without ever posting a client’s photograph it would rather hold back.

Fixed

Cream linen field, graphite plan, one amber-lit room, brass hairline, refined serif headline, hairline-sans annotations.

Variable

The plan itself, which room is lit, and the headline or project line.

Format rule

Landscape lays the plan beside the headline. Vertical places the plan below it with the lit room centered.

What it is not

An independent concept for a real business, created without its involvement. The artwork is AI-assisted and illustrative; the floor plan is invented and does not depict any client’s home or the studio’s work.

No commission, client approval or measured results exist for this work. Motion, print and email items in the rollout are proposals only.
