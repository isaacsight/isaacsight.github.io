Graza

Sign your dinner.

A spec campaign system for the olive oil that comes in a squeeze bottle. One rule, three colors and every format from a phone screen to a billboard: three vertical spots, a 21:9 billboard and a set of recipe cards.

Creative direction · Campaign system · Vertical video · Out-of-home · Recipe design · Copy
Three vertical spots · Spec concept · AI-generated footage, directed, designed and editedDownload all three (22.5 s)
01 / The briefInsight, idea, direction.

A drizzle is
handwriting.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The brand

Graza put olive oil in a squeeze bottle, a first for the category, with two oils: Sizzle for cooking and Drizzle for finishing. Its voice is casual and its look is playful.

The insight

The squeeze bottle turned olive oil from a pour into a gesture. A drizzle from a squeeze bottle is closer to handwriting than measuring, and everyone’s comes out a little different.

The idea

Sign your dinner. The finishing drizzle is your signature on the plate. Every piece of the campaign is one dish, shot from above, signed with one continuous line of oil.

01

The gesture, not the bottle.

Graza’s bottle is its signature and it protects it, so this concept never recreates it. Only a plain nozzle enters the frame. The oil line does the branding.

02

A system, not a hero.

One rule repeats across every format, so a new dish is a new ad in minutes and the feed reads as one campaign.

03

Food-color fields.

Tomato red, pea green and yolk yellow come from the dishes themselves, so color and food always agree.

02 / The systemFixed, variable, formats.
Tomato#C8372A
Pea#6F9A54
Yolk#E0A92A
Olive ink#1F3B1A

One dish. One line.
“Sign your ___.”

A condensed sans in caps for the verb and the dish, with a script “your” in between. The script is the only handwriting on the page, echoing the oil line it sits next to.

Fixed

Top-down dish, centered. One continuous drizzle line. The headline is always “Sign your ___.” Wordmark bottom-left. The nozzle only ever enters from the top edge.

Variable

The dish, the field color, and the shape of the signature, which is never drawn the same way twice.

Formats

9:16 spots (5.7 s squeeze, 1.8 s type), a 21:9 billboard, 4:5 recipe cards, and a 3×3 grid where each dish takes one row.

03 / Out-of-home21:9 billboard.

Sign your
tomatoes.

One row of heirloom tomatoes signed in a single loop of oil, running off the edge of the board. The headline gets the empty green on the left, so the line can be read from a moving car.

04 / Recipe cardsOne template, three dishes.

Every recipe
ends the same way.

Three steps, and step three is always the signature. The cards work as feed posts, as inserts in the box, and as printed cards at a tasting.

What it is not

An unsolicited spec concept, not commissioned or approved by Graza. All food footage, stills and music are AI-generated (fal.ai: nano-banana 2.1, minimax h3, stable-audio 2.5), then art directed, typeset, edited and finished by hand. Graza’s bottle, logo and illustrations are not shown or recreated; the wordmark is set in plain type.
