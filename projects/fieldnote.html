Brand identity · Campaign · Trade
01 / The ideaStart with a reason.

Let the ingredient list
lead the conversation.

The brief

Develop a juice identity that makes its contents easy to understand and turns ingredient clarity into a memorable campaign.

The response

The label borrows the calm logic of a field notebook: a recipe number, a name, and an orderly list of what is inside. A characterful serif adds warmth. The supporting typography stays direct, giving the ingredients room to be the message.

01

Build an editorial hierarchy.

A lowercase serif wordmark gives the brand a considered voice. A clear sans serif keeps the ingredient list straightforward.

02

Number the recipes.

Recipe numbers provide a simple organizing device that can carry from the bottle to a field guide, a social post and a retailer sheet.

03

Show the materials honestly.

Soft side light, paper texture and sliced produce create a quiet, tactile world around the product.

A label that reads
like a good note.

The identity separates expression from information: an organic serif for the name, a practical sans serif for the ingredients. The recipe number acts as a small but consistent navigation device.

Fixed

Recipe number, lowercase name, ingredient list. Forest green and paper remain the constant pair.

Variable

The recipe number and ingredients. Keep the list in the same position as the range grows.

Format rule

On the bottle, read name then recipe. In the campaign, enlarge the ingredients. On a trade sheet, make the information scannable.
