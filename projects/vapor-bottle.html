AURN

Packaging for a fictional fragrance: a brushed-aluminium bottle that comes out of thick white smoke.

AURN is an invented fragrance brand used to test a packaging-reveal format. A slim brushed-aluminium bottle with its name pressed into the metal stands on a wet black floor while smoke rolls away from it. Everything is black and white with a faint grid, so the only things that matter are the metal, the light and the smoke.

The idea.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The genre

Inspired by monochrome packaging reels where a metal bottle comes out of smoke over a grid and a wet floor.

The insight

Smoke is the oldest trick in fragrance: you are selling something invisible, so you show the air around it. Here the reveal is the product shot. The smoke clears and the bottle is just there, still and sharp. Brushed aluminium in monochrome keeps it cool and architectural instead of luxurious.

The idea

Packaging for a fictional fragrance: a brushed-aluminium bottle that comes out of thick white smoke.

01

Monochrome only

No colour at all, so metal grain, reflection and smoke density do the work. The grid overlay adds a quiet technical layer, like a packaging drawing.

02

Reveal by reversal

Rather than asking the model to make smoke clear and a crisp bottle appear, I generated smoke engulfing a sharp bottle and played it backwards. The clip lands on the clean original frame, so the final hero is never warped.

03

A short invented name

AURN is four letters that read well set vertically on a narrow bottle. It is fictional and not tied to any real brand.

03 / The processHow it was made.
STEP 01

Hero bottle

Generated the bottle in smoke on a wet floor with Nano Banana 2.1 (text to image, 9:16, 2K), black and white, with the name set vertically.

STEP 02

Detail and hand

Used Nano Banana 2.1 Edit for the wordmark macro and the in-hand shot (hand only, no face). The model engraved the name into the metal rather than raising it, so captions describe it as pressed or engraved.

STEP 03

Smoke reveal

Animated the hero with Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video: smoke rolling in until it nearly hides the bottle. Mid-clip the lettering softens under the smoke. Reversed, the clip clears onto the untouched first frame, followed by a slow push on that frame to reach 8 s.

STEP 04

Grid and type

Grid, labels and AI-GENERATED mark built in HTML, rendered with headless Chrome and composited in ffmpeg. The labels fade in as the smoke clears.
