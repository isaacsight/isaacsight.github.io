Mona Lynne

Make room for a little ritual.

A pop-art campaign for soaps and bath bombs — a grid of candy-coloured bathroom tiles, one fizzing sphere spilling foam across them, and a hand-painted sign for a headline.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Make room for a little ritual.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Mona Lynne makes handmade soaps and bath bombs in bright colours, the kind of product that is bought as much for the mood as for the wash.

The goal

Match the product’s colour with a campaign that is just as joyful, and tie it to one small idea: carving out a moment to pause.

The central idea

The bathroom wall as a pop-art canvas. A tile grid in four candy colours, one bath bomb fizzing out of its square, and the line painted across the tiles like a sign in a 1960s bathhouse. Bright, clean, a little surreal.

01

Tiles, not products.

The grid is the brand’s stage. The sphere is abstract, so the real products can star on the same tiles later.

02

Four candy colours.

Pink, tangerine, lilac and aqua, glossy with real reflections, lit flat and sunny so it reads clean rather than sugary.

03

Sign-painter type.

Chunky rounded letters in white with a plum shadow break across the grid like hand lettering; the CTA stays small in plum.

Glossy, gridded, buoyant.

A tile system: the grid is fixed, the colours rotate with the range, and one product per piece breaks the grid. Real bath bombs and soaps shot on tiles drop straight in.

Fixed

Tile grid, white sign type with plum shadow, brand name small in plum, CTA in plum.

Variable

Tile colours and the one thing that breaks the grid: a bomb, a bar, a splash.

Format rule

Landscape lets the foam spill sideways. Vertical stacks the headline and drops the sphere to the base.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
