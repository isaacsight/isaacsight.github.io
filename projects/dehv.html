DEHV

Scent meets sculpture.

A brutalist collection campaign for concrete candles — material, light and an oversized grotesk.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Scent meets sculpture.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

DEHV makes concrete candles and takes on custom projects. The vessels are the product: cast, architectural and reusable.

The goal

Position the collection the way the vessels deserve — as objects — and give wholesale and custom conversations a visual they can forward.

The central idea

If the vessel is sculpture, the campaign should behave like an architecture monograph. Concrete planes, one curved beam of persimmon light and a wordmark big enough to feel cast. The scent is implied by the light; the sculpture is implied by everything else.

01

Light is the only color.

Chalk grey and charcoal everywhere except one persimmon arc. It reads as warmth, as flame, and as the single accent a wholesale line sheet can reuse.

02

Type at architectural scale.

The condensed grotesk is set as tall as the image. Tiny grid labels around the board reference drawing conventions and keep the system feeling precise.

03

Split, don’t layer.

Image on one side, type on the other. In the vertical story the headline rotates to keep the same split — a move that becomes a recognizable brand behavior.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.
Chalk#E3E1DC
Concrete#8C8C88
Persimmon#FF4A1C
Charcoal#1D1D1B

Grid, grotesk, one accent.

A two-panel structure with a fixed type column. The image panel can take a real cast vessel, a material close-up or the abstract study used here; the type column never moves.

Fixed

Two-panel split. Wordmark top of the type column, headline below, CTA at the base. Grid labels at the board edges.

Variable

The image panel. Abstract planes now; approved product or process photography later.

Format rule

Landscape keeps type horizontal. Vertical rotates the headline 90° so the split survives a narrow frame.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
