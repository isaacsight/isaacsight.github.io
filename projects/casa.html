CASA

One design house, one palette, five objects that belong together.

CASA makes a chair, a fragrance and a piece of jewellery. Each object gets one continuous 10-second film in 9:16. Every film uses the same palette: warm grey, oxblood to violet anodized aluminium, brushed silver, travertine and palm-shadow light. Every film uses the same two-light studio setup and the same title: Helvetica Neue for the name, Menlo for the catalogue number. Each one ends on 'CASA · Nº 0X'.

Concept · World bible · Art direction · Prompting · Edit · Type
01 / The reelFive films joined with hard cuts, 42 s.

Six references.
One house.

Six Instagram references, each from a different genre (object archive, bent-metal furniture, 3D spec sheets, packaging in smoke, overhead interiors and an AI-built jewellery brand), become one brand world. Each film borrows one or two techniques. The palette, light and type are what make them one house.

02 / The films5 films · 9:16 · 10 s each

One action per film.
No cutaways, no fades.

Each film is either a single unbroken camera move or one continuous action covered from several angles with synced hard cuts, so the action never restarts. The catalogue title snaps on at the moment the object settles.

03 / The referencesGenre and technique, never the pieces.

What came from where.

The project started from six Instagram accounts in six different genres. Each film borrows one or two of their techniques, and the shared palette, light and type pull them into one brand.

@kura_archive (KURA)

Museum-archive framing: catalogue numbers in monospace, calm grey, macro surfaces. Used for the Nº system, the Nº 05 Archive plinth and the macro shot of the cuff in Nº 04.

@metastudio____ (metastudio)

Sculptural furniture bent from one tinted aluminium sheet, shot on a grey seamless sweep with a worn kilim. This is the Interchange chair in Nº 01 and Nº 02.

movastudio.3d

Objects moving with the precision of clean 3D, plus a product-spec register. Used for the rigid, exact rise of the bottle in Nº 03 and the subtitle lines.

harena.studio

Brushed-aluminium packaging rising from white smoke over a wet black floor, in monochrome. This is the bottle in Nº 03.

space.kadosh

A room seen from exactly overhead with raking sun and palm and blind shadows. This is Nº 02 Room.

yamatoskgc_ai

An AI-built luxury brand world (product, model and setting) with jewellery worn by a model whose face is never shown. This is Nº 04 Ember.

04 / The processHow every film is made.

Written first,
generated second.

Every film goes through the same pipeline. It is AI-generated from start to finish, then directed, checked frame by frame, rejected or kept, and assembled by hand.

STEP 01

The world bible

Before any image was made, one page set the palette (warm grey, oxblood to violet anodized aluminium, brushed silver, travertine, palm-shadow light), the lighting (exactly one key and one rim) and the type (Helvetica Neue for names, Menlo for catalogue numbers). Every still prompt is written as a Blender Cycles scene spec with a strict palette and an exclude list.

STEP 02

The chair as anchor

The Interchange chair still is passed back in as a reference image, so the same chair appears in the overhead room of Nº 02 and returns as a maquette beside the bottle and the cuff in Nº 05. That one recurring object is what makes five films feel like a single catalogue.

Nº 02 Room, frame by frame. GPT Image 2.6 edit with the chair as reference, then Kling 3.0 Pro.

STEP 03

One action, several angles

Vessel and Ember are each one continuous action covered by four shots in a single generation: wide, macro, low floor angle, then wide again. Because the shots are generated together, the motion carries across every cut.

Nº 03 Vessel: one rise, four synced angles. Kling 3.0 Pro multi-shot.

STEP 04

The takes that failed

Every clip is checked as a strip of frames before it is kept. The first Archive take asked for a turntable, and the chair maquette flattened and slid out of frame while the bottle stood still. It was replaced with a locked plinth and a slow camera push-in.

Rejected: the turntable take. The maquette loses its shape.

Approved: locked plinth, slow push-in.

STEP 05

Sound, type and edit

Only the model’s native foley is used, with no music and no voice, and every file is measured for level. Titles are set in HTML, rendered to transparent frames and snapped on with no fade. The reel joins all five films with hard cuts.

05 / The takeawayScope & notes.

A brand world is a set of rules. Hold the palette, the light and the type, and five very different objects read as one house.

What it is not

Concept, art direction and edit by Isaac Hernandez. Stills by OpenAI GPT Image 2.6 and motion by Kling v3 Pro, both via fal. The type and assembly were done in code (Chrome-rendered titles, ffmpeg). CASA is fictional. No real brands, products or people are depicted. The references are credited for genre and technique only.
