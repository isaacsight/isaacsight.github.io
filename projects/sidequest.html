SIDEQUEST

Take the
scenic snack.

A snack range and gift box built around the idea of a detour.

Brand identity · Packaging · PR gifting
01 / The ideaStart with a reason.

A snack break
worth wandering into.

The brief

Create a snack identity and gifting experience that turn a small everyday break into a moment of discovery.

The response

SIDEQUEST treats snacking as a welcome detour. The lettering is compact and playful; the long Q tail introduces a sense of movement. Cobalt and orange establish a bold contrast, while the warm cream type keeps the brand inviting.

01

Put personality in the letters.

The distinctive Q gives the wordmark a playful interruption. The name remains large and readable across the pouch range.

02

Create an opening sequence.

The gifting idea moves from a bold outer box to the assortment inside, then to a printed invitation to take a small detour.

03

Keep the flavor easy to find.

Large flavor names and a fixed category position give the playful system a practical information layer.

A little unexpected.
Everywhere it goes.

The stacked wordmark is the anchor. Contrasting color fields and a simple dotted route give the identity movement. The same few elements carry the pouch, the gift box and the campaign.

Fixed

Stacked name, cobalt, cream lettering and a dotted route. Leave a clear area for the flavor.

Variable

Flavor, accent color and the route across the format. Keep the route away from product information.

Format rule

Pouch: quick recognition. Box: a reveal. Insert: one invitation to take a detour.
