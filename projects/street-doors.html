Roll-Up

An old LA storefront gate rolls up onto a sunset beach.

A head-on phone shot of a weathered Spanish-revival doorway in LA, with a roll-up security gate covered in tags. For a moment the tags seem to shift. Then the gate rolls up like it does every morning, and behind it is the ocean at sunset, with warm light spilling onto the sidewalk.

['Art direction', 'VFX concept', 'Edit']
01 / The briefConcept and decisions.

The idea.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The genre

Inspired by street-level VFX shorts: real-looking everyday places with one impossible effect.

The insight

Street VFX works best when everything around the effect is boring and believable: a cracked sidewalk, a parking meter, flat late-afternoon sun. Roll-up gates are everywhere in LA and usually hide a closed shop. Here one opens onto the thing everyone in the city is always half-thinking about, the beach.

The idea

An old LA storefront gate rolls up onto a sunset beach.

01

Phone framing, head-on

The camera is at eye level, square to the doorway and nearly still, the way someone would film a door they walk past. Without camera moves to hide behind, the one impossible thing has to carry the piece.

02

A real mechanism for the reveal

No portal or glow. The gate rolls up into its housing like a real shutter, so the surprise comes from what's behind it and not from an effect. The sunset light reaching the sidewalk ties the beach into the street.

03

Light that already matches

The street is lit by warm low sun from the left, and the beach is at sunset. Because the two lights match, the cut between worlds feels possible and not pasted in.

03 / The processHow it was made.
STEP 01

Start frame

Generated a 9:16 photo of a weathered ornate LA doorway with a tagged roll-up gate in Nano Banana 2.1. The tags were prompted as abstract letterforms, with no readable names or brands.

STEP 02

End frame

Made the same view with the gate up and a sunset beach in the doorway using Nano Banana 2.1 Edit, which keeps the building, mouldings, sidewalk and meter in place.

STEP 03

Start-to-end video

Generated an 8 s clip with Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video from both frames, prompting the building to stay rigid, the tags to shift slightly and the gate to roll up like a real shutter. Frames were checked at nine points: no morphing, and the architecture stays stable. It was the only clip needed.

STEP 04

Finish

In ffmpeg: held the last frame for 1.2 s so the beach can breathe, added a small AI-GENERATED mark, and encoded to H.264 with faststart.

What it is not

All imagery is AI-generated: stills with Google Nano Banana 2.1 and Nano Banana 2.1 Edit, and motion with Alibaba Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video (start and end frame), all on fal. Edited in ffmpeg. The building is not a real address and the graffiti is invented letterforms. The video carries an AI-GENERATED mark. A self-initiated piece inspired by a genre, not a copy of any specific work.
