Verde Soapery

Made for the occasion.

A gifting campaign printed like wrapping paper — pressed flowers and eucalyptus in two-colour risograph, with the line cut across them on a green ribbon.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Made for the occasion.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Verde Soapery offers custom gift sets and private-label soap alongside its own range, which makes it as much a gifting business as a soap business.

The goal

Give the gifting and private-label offer its own campaign so a buyer planning a wedding, a corporate thank-you or a holiday order sees it as the main event.

The central idea

Every occasion gets its own set, so the campaign is the wrapping paper. A botanical print in two inks, slightly misregistered the way a risograph is, with the line printed across it on a ribbon. It looks like a gift before anything is opened.

01

Print, not product.

The botanicals are an abstract two-colour print. No boxes or labels are shown, so real sets can be photographed on the same paper later.

02

Two inks, one ribbon.

Forest green and blush on cream. The green diagonal panel is the ribbon and the headline sits on it.

03

Tall serif, cream.

A Didone-style serif knocked out of green reads as an invitation; the CTA stays small in the same green.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.
Forest#1F3B2C
Blush ink#E3A7B5
Cream stock#F1ECE2
Sage#9DB48F

Printed, botanical, giftable.

A wrapping-paper system: a two-ink botanical pattern per occasion, one diagonal ribbon panel, a tall serif headline. Weddings get blush, corporate gets cream, winter gets a deep red; the ribbon and the type never change.

Fixed

Brand name small at the top, headline on the ribbon panel, CTA below in green. Always two inks on cream.

Variable

The pattern and the second ink colour. Each occasion is its own print.

Format rule

Landscape runs the ribbon corner to corner. Vertical steepens the ribbon and stacks the headline.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
