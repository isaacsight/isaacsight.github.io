DAYGLOW

A little more
daylight.

A three-flavor soda range built around color and a stacked wordmark.

Brand identity · Packaging · Campaign
01 / The ideaStart with a reason.

An everyday ritual.
With the brightness turned up.

The brief

Create a botanical soda identity that feels optimistic and full of flavor, with a clear way to distinguish three recipes on a busy shelf.

The response

The idea starts with a small moment: stepping away from the day for something refreshing. That moment becomes the organizing principle. Sun-warmed color, compressed lettering and sharp afternoon shadows make the product feel like a little more daylight.

01

Make the name the asset.

A large stacked wordmark gives the narrow can a confident, recognizable front. Rounded counters soften its compressed structure.

02

Keep the system simple.

Each flavor gets a distinct field color. The brand position, category line and information order stay fixed across the range.

03

Let light do the storytelling.

Directional sunlight and real-looking ingredients give the photography energy without filling the frame with effects.

Three flavors.
One unmistakable family.

The structure is deliberately repeatable: wordmark, flavor, category, volume. The label color changes; the hierarchy holds. Supporting campaign type uses the same compact, generous shapes.

Fixed

Stacked name; category below the flavor; one dominant color per recipe.

Variable

Flavor name, pack color and ingredient pairing. Keep the light hard and directional.

Format rule

On a narrow pack, the name leads. In a poster, the campaign line leads and the pack acts as the signature.
