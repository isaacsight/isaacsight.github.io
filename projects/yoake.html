YOAKE

A konbini that runs on Tokyo time.

夜明け, dawn. A clay convenience store on a Kōenji backstreet that keeps living when you leave. The light follows the real clock in Tokyo, the street gets wet when it rains there, delivery trucks restock the shelves, and the limited items change with the season. Every package is designed. Pick something up and take the receipt home.

The subject

Japan's convenience stores never close. They have a rhythm: trucks at fixed times, onigiri restocked before the morning rush, seasonal flavours that last a few weeks, a cat that knows where the warm vending machine is.

The idea

A store that lives on real Tokyo time. Open it at lunch in Los Angeles and it's 3 a.m. in Kōenji: the street is dark, the shelves are thin, and the truck is due. Come back later and it's a different shop.

The brand

YOAKE is fictional: a crescent moon with the sun coming up under it, indigo and cream, persimmon and matcha. The packaging is the point of the piece, so it's drawn in code, with crisp type and no real brands.

03 / Making itfal, three.js, Blender.

Three tools,
one material.

The props (vending machine, truck, cat, bike, ATM, bins, coffee machine) were generated as clay-toy images with fal and turned into 3D models. The store itself is built in code, with every edge softened and every surface given the same generated clay texture, so the two read as one handmade set on one warm palette.

Blender bakes the soft shadows where things meet (shelves on the floor, the counter against the wall) into a single texture, which the browser then shows for free. The hero image is a full Cycles render of the same scene, exported from the live page at one moment.

04 / ShoppingCards, basket, receipt.

Tap a shelf.
Read the pack.

Every product opens a card with the same clay model turning slowly, its name in English and Japanese, the price before tax, and one true line about it. Add it to the basket, check out, and keep the receipt. Food to go is taxed at Japan's reduced 8%, the same as a real konbini.

How it was made

A personal concept. YOAKE is a fictional store; no real brands appear. Props were generated with fal (nano-banana 2.1, Hunyuan3D) and the sound with stable-audio 2.5. The store, packaging, behaviour and data are built in three.js, with the code written alongside an AI coding assistant under my direction. Soft shadows and the hero image come from Blender. Weather is live from Open-Meteo; delivery times are stylised. Foods and facts are real.
