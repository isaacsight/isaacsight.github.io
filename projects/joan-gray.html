Joan Gray

A memory, lit.

A gifting campaign that treats scent as memory — forest shadows on folded paper, not a product shot.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

A memory, lit.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Joan Gray Candles describes its work through the connection between scent and memory, and offers private-label and custom gifting alongside its core range.

The goal

Give the gifting offer a campaign of its own — one that a corporate buyer or a thoughtful friend would recognize as premium without a single candle being shown.

The central idea

A candle is a small way of keeping a memory. The campaign makes that literal: light moving across folded paper, a shadow of pine, a line that reads like the first words of a note. Everything stays suggestive so the real products can carry the final layer.

01

Metaphor over merchandise.

No fabricated vessels or wax. Double-exposed forest shadows and translucent paper stand in for scent, so the direction never misrepresents a real product.

02

One editorial serif.

A single refined serif carries brand, headline and CTA. Tracked-out capitals for the name; a soft italic for the call to action.

03

Keep the palette hushed.

Pine, parchment and a hint of bronze warmth. The story version crops in and stacks the headline so it reads at a glance on a phone.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.

Quiet, textured, editorial.

The system is built so it can hold real photography later: a textured, low-contrast field, a centered serif headline and a small italic CTA. Swap the paper study for an approved product still and the hierarchy holds.

Fixed

Centered headline, tracked brand name at the top, italic CTA at the base. Generous margins on every format.

Variable

The background study. Shadow, paper, candlelight or an approved product photograph can occupy the field.

Format rule

Landscape leads with the full line on one row. Vertical stacks the headline and crops tighter into the texture.
