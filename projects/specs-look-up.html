Look up.

The world in one ink.
Everything you need in the other.

An unsolicited poster concept for Specs, Snap's AR glasses. In two-ink printing, a third colour only appears where the inks overlap. That's what the glasses do: the real world, a computed layer on top, and the useful part where they meet.

Unsolicited concept · not affiliated with Snap Inc. · AI-assisted under my direction
