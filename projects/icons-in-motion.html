LA Stool 01

An original stool made of three nested tubes in lifeguard-tower colours, turning inside a technical spec sheet.

LA Stool 01 is an original design object presented in the style of a design-classics motion series: the object assembles and rotates on light grey while a spec sheet frames it. Three bent steel tubes in sky blue, sun yellow and coral red nest inside one another under a round ash seat. The colours come from the painted lifeguard towers along LA beaches.

01 / The briefConcept and decisions.

The idea.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The genre

Inspired by design-classic motion series that pair a rotating 3D object with a precise technical spec sheet.

The insight

Design-classic reels make objects feel canonical by pairing slow rotation with dry technical type. I wanted to give an invented LA object that same treatment, as if it already sat in a museum catalogue. Nested tubes keep the build legible: you can count the parts as it turns.

The idea

An original stool made of three nested tubes in lifeguard-tower colours, turning inside a technical spec sheet.

01

Three tubes, three colours

Each tube is its own colour, so the structure explains itself: the blue, yellow and coral loops read as separate parts even mid-rotation.

02

The spec sheet does the talking

Thin rules, small-caps labels in Helvetica and figures in Menlo. The type is built in HTML and composited, so every word is exact and editable, not generated.

03

Honest labelling

The designer line says '(concept)' and the sheet is footed 'AI-generated concept · not in production', so it is never mistaken for a real product.

03 / The processHow it was made.
STEP 01

Object render

Generated the assembled stool with Nano Banana 2.1 (text to image, 9:16, 2K), keeping the object in the middle 40% of the frame to leave room for the sheet.

STEP 02

Exploded view

Made the exploded state with Nano Banana 2.1 Edit. The first try was rejected because the tubes changed shape (hooked feet). A second, stricter prompt kept them closer, though the blue frame still reads as a straight-legged ring rather than a loop.

STEP 03

Rotation

Animated the assembled render with Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video as a turntable with a locked camera. Frames were checked: the three loops and seat hold their shape through the turn. Slowed 1.6x with motion interpolation, then crossfaded from the exploded still so the parts appear to settle into place.

STEP 04

Spec sheet

Laid out the sheet (PRODUCT, DESIGNER, YEAR, SIZE, STRUCTURE, SERIES, part number (01)) in HTML, rendered it with headless Chrome and overlaid it in ffmpeg.
