A little company

A hand-drawn character loop, generated with fal and timed on twos.

A shaggy green creature and a tiny white pigeon on flat sunset orange. The pigeon flutters up and lands on the creature's head, and the creature breaks into a smile. It looks drawn by hand, with scratchy ink and boiling lines, but the frame and the motion are generated.

The genre

Inspired by hand-drawn character loops with boiling lines on flat colour.

The insight

Hand-drawn loops feel warm because the lines never sit still. I directed an image model to draw the characters in naive ink and an image-to-video model to animate them with boiling lines, then stepped the result to 12 fps so it moves like drawings on twos.

The idea

A hand-drawn character loop, generated with fal and timed on twos.

01

Naive ink, flat colour

Thick uneven outlines, scratchy fur hatching and one flat background keep it reading as a drawing, not 3D.

02

On twos

The generated 30 fps clip is stepped to 12 drawings per second, which brings back the handmade stutter.

03

One small beat

Hop, land, smile. A loop works when it is one gesture, not a story.

03 / The processHow it was made.
STEP 01

First try, in code

I first built the loop in SVG with a turbulence filter for the line boil. It worked, but it looked like an imitation, so I scrapped it and generated the real thing.

STEP 02

Draw the characters

Nano Banana 2.1 drew the creature and pigeon on flat orange, in naive marker and ink.

STEP 03

Animate two takes

The same frame went to LTX 2.5 Pro and Wan 3.0 Prime. The LTX take smeared the eye into a mouth near the end; the Wan take held the drawing for the whole clip, so it was used.

STEP 04

Step and title

ffmpeg drops the clip to 12 fps on a 24 fps timeline and adds a small title.
