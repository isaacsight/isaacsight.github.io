Overhead Rooms

Four small Los Angeles rooms seen from straight above, where the only thing that moves is the light.

A series of exactly top-down interiors: a reading daybed, a terrazzo bathroom, a concrete patio and a drafting studio. Each one is mostly empty floor, crossed by hard late sun, palm fronds and venetian-blind stripes. The stills, two light-only loops and a 16-second sequence are all AI-generated.

01 / The briefConcept and decisions.

The idea.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The genre

Inspired by AI top-down interior photography ('simple space, perfect feeling' minimalism), not a copy of any creator's images.

The insight

LA light is the real subject of most LA rooms: at four in the afternoon the blinds and the palms draw on the floor. Looking straight down turns each room into a flat composition of objects and shadow, so the furniture can stay simple and the light does the work.

The idea

Four small Los Angeles rooms seen from straight above, where the only thing that moves is the light.

01

Exactly overhead, nothing else

Every frame is a strict bird's-eye view. With no perspective, the rooms read like floor plans with weather, and the four images sit together as one set.

02

Only the light moves

In the moving shots the camera is locked and the objects stay still. The palm shadows sway, the sun band drifts and steam rises from a coffee cup. Nothing else happens, which keeps the calm of the stills.

03

One quiet line per room

Each room gets a short lowercase caption in a small sans, set low on the frame in dark ink so it reads on bright floors without a box behind it.

03 / The processHow it was made.
STEP 01

Four rooms from text

Each room was generated as a 9:16 still with Nano Banana 2.1 from a scene prompt: an exact top-down view, the room's few objects, and raking sun through blinds or palms. All four were kept from the first pass. In the reading room the book hides the reader's face.

STEP 02

Light-only motion

Three clips were made with Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video, prompted for a locked camera and rigid objects. The patio (swaying palm shadows, coffee steam) and bathroom (drifting sun band) clips held their shape. The bathroom clip drifts slightly in its first half-second, so that part is trimmed.

STEP 03

Rejected take: the page turn

The reading-room clip was rejected. During the page turn the book narrowed and showed the edge of the reader's face, which broke the no-faces rule. That room appears in the sequence as a slow push on the still.

STEP 04

Sequence and type

The 5-second clips were slowed with frame blending into 6–7 second loops. The 16-second sequence cuts between the four rooms with crossfades, using slow pushes on the two stills. Captions were set in HTML, rendered with headless Chrome and composited in ffmpeg.

What it is not

All imagery is AI-generated: stills with Google Nano Banana 2.1, motion with Alibaba Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video (via fal), edit and type with ffmpeg and headless Chrome. No real rooms or people are shown. A self-initiated piece inspired by a genre, not a copy of any specific work.
