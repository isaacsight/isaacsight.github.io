Horchata Cold Brew

A bright illustrated 3D spot for an invented drink, built around one whipped-cream pour.

An original iced drink, horchata cold brew, shown in the punchy flat-graphic 3D style of a café ad. A cartoon hand pours whipped cream, it coils into a swirl, cinnamon falls, and the name lands. The cup band uses an original folk-art stripe pattern.

01 / The briefConcept and decisions.

The idea.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The genre

Inspired by bright illustrated 3D food and coffee spots built around a whipped-cream pour; original drink, pattern and art direction.

The insight

Horchata and cold brew are both everyday LA drinks, and putting them together makes an obvious menu special. I treated it like a real launch spot: one hero action (the pour), one colour field (cobalt blue) and one pattern (the stripe band) people would remember from across the room.

The idea

A bright illustrated 3D spot for an invented drink, built around one whipped-cream pour.

01

One action, one colour

The whole piece is built around the pour on a single saturated blue, so nothing pulls focus from the cream.

02

Stripes as identity

Instead of a logo, the cup carries an original band of magenta, orange, yellow, green and turquoise stripes. It works as the drink's brand mark.

03

Illustrated, not photoreal

Clean shapes, soft gradients and four-point sparkles keep it cheerful and graphic, like an animated ad rather than a product photo.

03 / The processHow it was made.
STEP 01

Hero cup

Generated the finished drink with Nano Banana 2.1 (9:16): a layered horchata and coffee iced drink with a cream swirl, cinnamon and the stripe band, on cobalt blue.

STEP 02

Before-the-pour frame

Used Nano Banana 2.1 Edit on the hero to remove the cream and add a cartoon hand tipping a white pitcher, keeping the cup, band and background consistent.

STEP 03

Start-to-end animation

Animated from the pre-pour frame to the hero with Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video (8 s, 9:16): the ribbon pours, piles into the swirl, cinnamon dusts on and the stick drops in. The first take was used. Frame checks showed the cup and band holding their shape.

STEP 04

End card

Held the last frame and faded in the product name with HTML rendered in headless Chrome, composited in ffmpeg. A third edit produced the close-up still.

What it is not

All imagery and motion are AI-generated. Stills: Google Nano Banana 2.1 and Nano Banana 2.1 Edit via fal. Motion: Alibaba Wan 3.0 Prime image-to-video (start and end frame) via fal. Type and compositing: HTML/headless Chrome and ffmpeg. The drink and its packaging are fictional, with no real brand. A self-initiated piece inspired by a genre, not a copy of any specific work.
