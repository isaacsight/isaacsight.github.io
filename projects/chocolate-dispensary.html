Chocolate Dispensary

Find your next favorite.

A discovery campaign that treats craft chocolate like an independent bookstore — a shelf of spines, one pulled forward.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Find your next favorite.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

The Chocolate Dispensary curates craft chocolate the way an independent bookstore curates titles — by taste, origin and maker, with staff picks.

The goal

Make discovery the offer. Give the shop a campaign that says ‘browse’ rather than ‘buy’, and that can carry staff picks and new arrivals every month.

The central idea

If the shop is a bookstore, the bars are spines. The hero illustrates a shelf of unlabeled, color-blocked spines with tiny handwritten index tabs and one pulled forward — the moment of finding the one you didn’t know you wanted.

01

Spines, not bars.

A flat illustration of abstract spines keeps every maker anonymous and nothing misrepresented. Real wrappers can replace them in a later round.

02

Bookish typography.

A classic serif for the headline, small caps for the shop name and a typewriter face for the CTA — the furniture of a good bookshop.

03

Kraft as the brand field.

One warm paper color across hero and story. The spines supply all the color; the field stays calm.

A shelf that changes every month.

The shelf is the template. ‘Staff pick’, ‘new arrivals’ and ‘single origin’ each become a shelf with one spine pulled forward and a different index tab — the same picture, a new reason to come in.

Fixed

Kraft field, small-caps shop name, serif headline, typewriter CTA, illustrated shelf with exactly one spine pulled forward.

Variable

Spine colors and count, the index-tab text, and which spine is pulled. Headline changes with the monthly theme.

Format rule

Landscape puts type left and the shelf right, bleeding off the edge. Vertical stacks type over a contained shelf.

Project scope & production notes

What this study covers

Created as a complimentary, self-initiated concept and shared with the business as a proposal.

What it is not

An independent concept for a real business, created without its involvement. The artwork is AI-assisted and illustrative; the spines are abstract and do not depict any maker’s bars or the shop’s premises.

No commission, client approval or measured results exist for this work. Motion, print and email items in the rollout are proposals only.
