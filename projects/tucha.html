TuCha

A sweeter pause.

A tea-and-dessert campaign built from steam, a clay arch and a gradient that moves from matcha to milk tea.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

A sweeter pause.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

TuCha tells the story of tea evolving from raw ingredient to modern ritual, pairing drinks with desserts.

The goal

Turn ‘tea and dessert’ into a moment people can picture — a short, sweet break — so every seasonal drink has a campaign home.

The central idea

The pause is the product. Steam is the only ‘ingredient’ shown; a single clay arch stands in for the cup, the counter, the doorway. The gradient carries the menu — matcha, cream, milk tea — without naming a single drink.

01

Steam instead of drinks.

No cups or desserts, so nothing is passed off as the real menu. Steam reads instantly as warmth and ritual.

02

One rounded sans.

A geometric sans with soft terminals in warm clay. It sits between tea-house calm and modern café energy.

03

Gradient as menu.

Green, cream and clay in one wash. Seasonal drinks shift the balance of the gradient rather than changing the layout.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.
Matcha#8FAA6D
Cream#FBF4EA
Clay#B86A45
Ink#2B2B26

Steam, arch, gradient.

Three elements and a headline. A new drink or dessert gets a new gradient balance and a new line; the arch and the steam stay, which is what makes the series recognizable on a feed.

Fixed

Tracked wordmark top-left, rounded-sans headline, arch at the base with steam rising from it, underlined CTA.

Variable

Gradient balance (greener, creamier, warmer) and the headline for the season or the pairing.

Format rule

Landscape keeps the headline left and the arch right. Vertical centers both and lets the steam run the full height.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.

What it is not

An independent concept for a real business, created without its involvement. The artwork is AI-assisted and illustrative; it does not depict TuCha’s drinks, desserts or premises.

No commission, client approval or measured results exist for this work. Motion, print and email items in the rollout are proposals only.
