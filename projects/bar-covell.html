Covell

Choose an adjective.

A typographic campaign for a Los Feliz wine bar with no written list — the bartender’s own question becomes the headline, and a glass fills with words.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Choose an adjective.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Bar Covell is a neighborhood wine and beer bar in Los Feliz with no written list; the bartenders ask what you’re in the mood for — “choose an adjective” — and pour from there.

The goal

Put the bar’s one unusual habit on a poster, so the thing regulars already love is the thing new neighbors hear first.

The central idea

The campaign is the bar’s own line. A glass holds words instead of wine — bright, funky, serious, cheap, weird, Tuesday — in handwritten red ink. The headline is literally what the bartender will say to you.

01

Words, not bottles.

No labels, no bottles, no interior. The glass-of-adjectives device says ‘no list, just talk to us’ without photographing anything.

02

Handwriting in wine red.

The adjectives are hand-lettered and slightly uneven, like a chalkboard. The headline is a classic serif so the joke has something formal to land on.

03

One stain.

A single wine-ring on the cream field. It’s the only ‘photo’ in the campaign and it’s the right one.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.
Cream#F4EAD8
Wine#5A1A22

Chalk#2B2622

A glass full of whatever you said.

The glass is the template and the adjectives are the content. A weeknight post fills it with ‘Tuesday’; a natural-wine night fills it with ‘funky’; a holiday fills it with whatever the room is saying. The serif headline never changes.

Fixed

Cream field, glass silhouette, hand-lettered red adjectives, serif headline, one wine-ring stain.

Variable

Which adjectives fill the glass, and a small chalk-style line for the occasion.

Format rule

Landscape puts the glass beside the headline. Vertical puts the headline above a taller glass.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.

What it is not

An independent concept for a real business, created without its involvement. The artwork is AI-assisted and illustrative; it does not depict Bar Covell’s interior, staff or wines.

No commission, client approval or measured results exist for this work. Motion, print and email items in the rollout are proposals only.
