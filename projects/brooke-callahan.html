Brooke Callahan

Dyed by hand. Cut to move.

A color-drop campaign for an Echo Park label that designs, dyes and sews in-house — dye-bath fields bleeding into each other.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Dyed by hand. Cut to move.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Brooke Callahan is an independent womenswear label whose pieces are designed, dyed and sewn by a small team in Echo Park, in a wide, confident color range.

The goal

Turn the label’s two real differentiators — hand-dyeing and a bold palette — into a campaign format that can announce every new color drop.

The central idea

Show the dye, not the dress. Four of the label’s colors bleed into each other the way wet dye moves on fabric, pooling and feathering at the edges. The headline names the two things the studio does by hand.

01

Dye as the image.

Color fields with soft, wet edges are unmistakably about process, and they never fabricate a garment or a model.

02

Palette straight from the rack.

Turquoise, purple, chocolate, yellow — pulled from the actual colorways, so the campaign and the shop agree.

03

Geometric sans in cream.

A confident, slightly wide sans knocked out of the color. It holds its own against saturated fields without shouting.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.
Turquoise#2AA7A0
Purple#4A2A7A
Chocolate#5A3A2A
Yellow#E8C73A

Four colors in. One drop out.

The border and the type are fixed; the dye field changes with every drop. Over a season the posts become a swatch book of what the studio has been mixing.

Fixed

Cream border, bleeding dye field, geometric sans headline in cream, small tracked brand name.

Variable

The colors in the field (the drop’s colorways) and the headline.

Format rule

Landscape lets the colors run left-to-right. Vertical stacks them top-to-bottom so each color gets a band.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
