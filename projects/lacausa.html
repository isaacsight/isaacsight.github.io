Lacausa

Made here. Worn everywhere.

A process-led campaign for a made-in-LA womenswear label — sun-washed linen and late-afternoon light instead of a lookbook.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

Made here. Worn everywhere.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

LACAUSA makes relaxed California womenswear in downtown Los Angeles, producing locally in small batches whenever possible.

The goal

Make ‘made in Los Angeles’ feel like a quality, not a footnote — a campaign for the process pages and new-arrival moments that doesn’t depend on a model shoot.

The central idea

The light is the location. Hard afternoon sun on linen, a stucco wall, long shadows — California without a palm tree. The fabric is shown as fabric, so the campaign is honest about being about how the clothes are made rather than what they look like on.

01

Textile, not garment.

Folded cloth in the label’s palette stands in for the collection. No garments or models are fabricated; a real campaign shoot can drop into the same light.

02

One serif, wide.

A calm, wide-tracked serif in ink. It reads as considered and slow — the brand’s own words — rather than fashion-loud.

03

Shadow as texture.

The long shadows do the styling. Keep them hard; they are what makes the image feel like 4 p.m. in Los Angeles.

Light, cloth, one line.

A texture field in afternoon light, a serif line and a small CTA. Each new drop changes the cloth colors; each process story changes the line. The light stays the same, which is the brand.

Fixed

Hard warm directional light, textile field, wide-tracked serif, small-caps brand name, one-line CTA.

Variable

Fabric colors (the season’s palette), the headline, and whether the field is cloth or an approved photograph.

Format rule

Landscape lets the folds run horizontally under the headline. Vertical turns the folds upright and drops the headline into the shadow.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.

Project scope & production notes

What it is not

An independent concept for a real business, created without its involvement. The artwork is AI-assisted and illustrative; it does not depict LACAUSA’s garments, studio or makers.

No commission, client approval or measured results exist for this work. Motion, print and email items in the rollout are proposals only.
