Oura Ring 5

Your heart keeps score.

Five hook-first vertical ads for Cardio Effort on Oura Ring 5. Each opens on one word, a single weekly effort band, then shows the ordinary moment that earned it. Five bands, five ads: the variant test is built into the idea.

Creative direction · Paid social system · Copy · Motion · Edit
01 / The briefInsight, idea, direction.

Your heart doesn’t
know it’s a workout.
Why this idea, for this brand.

The launch

Cardio Effort (Oct 20, 2026) scores how hard each workout works your heart and totals it for the week in five bands: Light, Fair, Maintaining, Building and Reaching. At Reaching, Oura suggests it “may be time to prioritize recovery.” It runs on Oura Ring 5.

The insight

Nobody’s week looks like a training plan. A dog walk, a bike commute and the stairs count too, and the ring already knows.

The idea

Your heart keeps score. Lead with the score, not the ring. The first frame is one huge word on that band’s colour; the second is the real-life moment; the last names the feature.

01

Hook in the first second.

Each ad opens on a single word on a flat colour field. It stops the scroll and makes the viewer ask “why Reaching?” before the picture answers.

02

Five bands, five ads.

The feature’s own structure becomes the testing matrix: same template, five hooks, five moments, five colours from cool morning to hot late sun.

03

Keep the ring honest.

The ring stays small and true to size on a real hand, a plain band with no logo. Hands only, no faces, no glamour product shot.

02 / The adsFive 9:16 variants, about 6 s each.

Hook, moment,
feature.

Every ad follows the same three beats so results can be compared: a one-word hook on colour, the moment with the line, then the end card. Only the band changes. They play muted with captions burned in, because that is how they will be watched.

03 / The takeawayScope & notes.

The product is a ring, but the ads sell a number your heart already earned. That is a reason to wear it every day, not only on gym days.
