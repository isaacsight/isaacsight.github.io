Milla

A gift, beautifully considered.

A gifting campaign that treats the box like a gallery object — faceted geometry in cocoa, blush and one plane of gold.

Campaign direction · Hero + story design · Rollout plan
01 / The briefGoal, idea, direction.

A gift, beautifully considered.
Why this line, for this business.

The business

Milla Chocolates makes geometric, fine-art chocolates and presents them with the care of a gallery piece.

The goal

Give gifting its own campaign — something a corporate buyer or a careful gift-giver reads as considered before they ever see a flavor list.

The central idea

A considered gift is an object first. The hero stages abstract faceted solids the way a museum would stage a sculpture: hard light, a stone plinth, one gold plane. It borrows the brand’s geometry without ever pretending to be the product.

01

Sculpture, not confection.

Abstract faceted forms carry the geometry so the direction is honest about being illustrative. Approved product photography can take the same plinth later.

02

Gallery-catalogue type.

A quiet modern serif at modest scale, with the brand tracked wide above it. The CTA is a single small line — the restraint is the luxury cue.

03

Image as a window.

The photograph sits inside a cream card rather than bleeding to the edge, which reads as print and frames the object like a plate in a catalogue.

02 / The visual systemColor, type, hierarchy.
Cocoa#4A2B25
Blush#D9A9A1
Gold leaf#C9A24A
Cream card#F3E9E3

Object. Plinth. One line.

A framed image panel, a serif headline to its left (or above, in vertical) and a single CTA. Seasonal gifting swaps the object and the gold accent; the card and the hierarchy never change.

Fixed

Cream card with an inset image panel. Tracked brand name top-left, serif headline, one-line CTA at the base.

Variable

The object in the panel — abstract geometry now, an approved product still later — and the accent metal.

Format rule

Landscape puts type beside the image. Vertical stacks headline over image and moves the CTA below it.

03 / The completed boardHero + story, as delivered.

Two formats.
One identity.

The landscape hero and the vertical story were designed together so the campaign reads the same on a website banner and on a phone. This is the completed, deliverable artwork; everything in section 05 builds on it.
