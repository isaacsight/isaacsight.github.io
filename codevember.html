Codevember

Ink, paper and flaps,
made in code.

Codevember is a month of small pieces made in code. Each one borrows a real material, such as a two-ink riso print, a cyanotype, ink floating on water or a station board, and follows its rules instead of faking it with effects. Each is a single idea you get in a few seconds, and each one does something when you touch it.

04 · Tool · Your photo

Riso Press

Choose any photo and it is printed the way a risograph would print it: blue takes the shadows and cool tones, pink takes the warm light, each as a grain of dots. The plates go down one after the other, a little out of line. Set your own headline, pick a shape and download the print.

Colour separation · Stochastic dither · Misregistration
Press your photo ↗
07 · Marbling · Drop and comb

Float

Suminagashi, the Japanese way of marbling. Drops of black, vermilion and indigo float on water and push each other into rings, then a comb drags them into feathers. Nothing is painted, only moved. Click to drop ink, drag to comb.

Aubrey Jaffer’s marbling equations · Three inks on water
Marble some ink ↗
05 · Paper · Drag to open

Fold

A two-ink poster folded into four. It opens one panel at a time, showing the blank back of the sheet as it turns, and the line only makes sense once it is fully open. Drag down to unfold it yourself.

Paper physics in 2D · Flat shading, no effects
Unfold it ↗
06 · Cyanotype · Click for a new fern

Sun Print

A cyanotype. A fern and cut-paper letters are laid on a brushed sheet and left in the sun. The paper turns grey-green where the light reaches, and after the rinse the shadows come up white on Prussian blue.

Contact print · Exposure and rinse
Print in the sun ↗
02 · Motion · Stamps on the beat

Payday

A paycheck set in blue. Rent, phone, insurance, groceries and streaming land on it as pink stamps, one per beat, and the balance only moves when a stamp hits. It ends below zero.

Kinetic type · Land, then hold still
Watch it land ↗
08 · Split-flap · Type a message

Departures

A split-flap board where every letter flips through the alphabet to reach the next. Today’s departures are a day: sunrise on time, the inbox delayed, the nap gone. Type a message and press Enter to put it on the board.

Mechanical type · Black, warm white and one amber
Board it ↗
03 · Pen plotter · Type your own word

By Hand

The words BY HAND filled in by two pens, the way a plotter draws: pink one way, blue the other, purple where they cross. Then the answer lands underneath. Type a word and press Enter to plot your own.

Hatching · Plotter path order
Plot a word ↗
01 · Registration · Drag the plate

Out of Line

The same words on two plates. The blue plate slips out of register on the beat and locks back, so you can see where the third colour comes from. Drag it yourself and let go.

Overprint · Misregistration
Pull it out of line ↗

How they were made

Process

I wrote the idea for each piece and the rules it had to follow. An AI coding agent wrote the code against my studio rules, and I checked every frame and signed off. The videos are rendered frame by frame from the live pieces, so what you see is what runs in the browser.

The sample photo in Riso Press is a crop from my LOW TIDE concept, which was made with an image model under my direction. Everything else, including the type, the ferns and the marbling, is drawn in code.
