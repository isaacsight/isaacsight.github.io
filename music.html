Music

Three songs,
sun-up to late night.

Original songs written and produced in Ableton Live: a summer anthem, a late-night disco cut and a dream-pop slow burn. Each one is built around a single riff you can hum after one listen, and each has its own cover art.

Paper Planets

A slow-burning dream-pop anthem. A kalimba riff through wobbly tape echo, harp glissandos into each chorus, a choir pad and half-time drums that open up into a big finish.

For fans of Empire of the Sun

These are instrumental demos; the lead vocals are still to come. I built them with a custom bridge that lets an AI assistant work inside Ableton Live alongside me, writing parts, swapping instruments and mixing, while the songs, the riffs and every decision stayed mine. The covers are drawn from each song’s hook: every bar, slice and planet is placed by a real note, then printed in paper and ink.
