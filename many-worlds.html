Many Worlds.

One man walks through a door on a Los Angeles sidewalk and keeps walking, through eighteen worlds. The world is rebuilt in a new medium every few seconds. He never changes, and every cut lands on his stride.

Production notesMade in one afternoon

The hardest thing in AI video is keeping one character the same from shot to shot. So that became the whole film: change everything except him.

What I did, and what AI made

What I did

I came up with the character and the idea, wrote the rules, chose every frame and directed the edit.

What AI made

Claude Fable 5.2 wrote the shot prompts and built the edit as code in HyperFrames. The images, motion, music and sound were generated on fal (Seedream 5 Pro, Gemini Omni Flash, Kling 4, ElevenLabs). Every still starts from one master image of the character. No client, no stock footage, no real people.
