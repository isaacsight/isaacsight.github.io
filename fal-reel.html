One sentence — a lighthouse keeper in an orange oilskin as a storm rolls in — sent through the fal.ai stack: four stills, three edits that keep the same keeper, image-to-video, a score and storm ambience, a 1080p→4K upscale, wireframe/clay/charcoal re-renders, direct text-to-video, and twelve variations at once. How it was madeOne prompt → nine chapters

Text → image (google/nano-banana-2.1) → edit → image-to-video (minimax/h3-max) → text-to-audio (stable-audio-25) → upscale (topaz/precision) → re-render → text-to-video → variations.

Production notes

What this is

A self-initiated capabilities reel. Seven stills, sixteen six-second videos, one 4K upscale and two audio beds were generated on fal.ai from one seed sentence and its edits; the chapter slates show the exact model for each step. Composited, graded and cut in-house with the type animated in the composite.

What it isn’t

Not affiliated with or endorsed by fal.ai; the models are credited as used. No stock footage, licensed music or client work. The keeper is a generated figure, not a real person.
