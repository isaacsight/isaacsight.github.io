Spec concept for HeyGen VoiceNovember 20260:25 · with sound

Recorded / Typed.

A launch video I would pitch for HeyGen Voice. You hear me say one line twice. The first time is a real recording. The second time is my HeyGen digital twin, speaking words I typed.

Concept · Art direction · Edit

The ideaSo what?

If the voice in a launch video sounds fake, the product looks fake. So the video is a test. Which one was recorded?

It is made for a marketing or training lead who has stayed away from avatar video because of the voice. They hear the proof before they hear a claim.

Three openings to testOne template · only the headline changes

Each version is a re-render of the same file and takes about fifteen seconds. The footage, the voice and the timing are identical, so a test measures the headline and nothing else.

How I would judge itCompared across the three openings
  • 01How many viewers are still watching at three seconds.
  • 02How many click through to try Voice.
  • 03How many start a trial.
Production notesMade in one evening, including a re-shoot

My first digital twin came from a bad webcam recording: camera too low, a ceiling light in the shot. No setting fixed it. Raising the camera to eye level and facing a plain wall did.

What I did, and what AI made

What I did

I came up with the idea, wrote the script and recorded myself once, for 86 seconds. I set the layout, the two colours and the timing, checked every frame, and re-shot the source when the first twin looked wrong.

What AI made

HeyGen built my digital twin and cloned my voice from that one recording, then generated the 17-second clip with its own voice model. Claude Fable 5.2 ran HeyGen through its MCP connector from Claude Code and built the edit as code in HyperFrames. The two soundwaves are drawn from the real audio of each take. Take A is unedited apart from its volume.
