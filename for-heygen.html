Recorded / Typed.

A launch video I would pitch for HeyGen Voice. You hear me say one line twice. The first is a real recording. The second is my HeyGen digital twin, speaking words I typed.

02Original reel1:18 · with sound

Many Worlds.

One man walks through eighteen worlds: paper, clay, underwater, wool, the Moon, pixels, chalk. The world changes every few seconds. He never does, and every cut lands on his stride.

Why it's here: keeping one character the same across eighteen looks is the same problem as keeping a brand's videos consistent at volume.

03Spec campaigns reel1:30 · with sound

One line.

One acid-green line travels through four spec campaigns and becomes each of them: a whisk swirl, a dawn run, a sardine's wake, a signature in olive oil.

Why it's here: it is one system, built in HyperFrames, with each brand as a row of data. My first cut used full-frame AI footage and looked generic, so I rebuilt it around type and colour and kept the footage small.

04Brand spot made only of code0:14 · with sound

FIBS.

A spot for a fictional jelly sweet that never pretended to be fruit. It is 100% artificial and proud of it. So is the ad: no camera, no footage and no image, only code.

Why it's here: a short brand spot with one clear idea, where the way it was made is part of the joke.

Three things I would make firstIdeas, not promises
  • 01A look for each sprint.One template system per two-month sprint, so every feature video that ships in it reads as part of one campaign.
  • 02An event kit.For each event or webinar: a promo, a personal thank-you from the speaker's avatar, and a recap, all from one template.
  • 03Blog to YouTube.A repeatable format that turns one blog post into a two-minute video.
